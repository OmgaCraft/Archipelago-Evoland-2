# Evoland 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every chest, quest reward and item picked up from the ground (143 locations) can hold an item from
the multiworld: Stars, Orikon Ore, Maana, cards, and also story items such as keys, the Bombs, the
Stone of Time, the Boat, the Control Terminal and the five Magi Key fragments.

When you open a randomized chest, the message "Objet envoyé dans le multiworld Archipelago !"
appears instead of the original item. Items you receive arrive with the usual "item obtained"
animation as soon as your hero is free.

A few special cases keep their original behaviour: the prison chest that gives back your confiscated
weapon, Mr Mudwell's gold deposit, and shop purchases. Old Pol, the fisherman of Nibiru, always gives
the Old House Key: leaving the village requires opening the chest in that house.

## What is the goal?

Defeat the final boss. Optionally, the way to the final boss can require a number of Stars.

## Which versions of the game are supported?

Evoland 2 inside Evoland Legendary Edition (Steam).
